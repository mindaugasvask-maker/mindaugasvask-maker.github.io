MV Digital is a small digital marketing consultancy managing Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns for small and medium-sized businesses, mainly in the Baltics.

MV Digital Ads Tool

MV Digital Ads Tool is an internal application used only by MV Digital staff to report on and manage advertising campaigns in Google Ads and Meta accounts that clients have authorised us to manage. It has no public users or sign-up.

The tool requests access to the Google Ads API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords) solely to read campaign performance data and make account management changes approved by MV Digital staff.
