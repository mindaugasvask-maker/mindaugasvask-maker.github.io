Privacy Policy

Application: MV Digital Ads Tool · Operator: MV Digital · Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how MV Digital collects, uses and protects data accessed through MV Digital Ads Tool, an internal tool used to manage and report on advertising campaigns on Google Ads and Meta platforms for client businesses. It has no public users and no public sign-up.

2. Data we access

Only for advertising accounts that a client has authorised MV Digital to manage, the tool may access: campaign, ad group, ad, keyword and budget settings; aggregated performance metrics (cost, impressions, clicks, conversions); audience definitions; and, where the client runs lead ads and asks us to process them, lead form submissions. Through Google sign-in the tool receives the signed-in MV Digital staff member's email address to identify the account used.

3. How we use data

To create, manage and optimise advertising campaigns on behalf of the client, to prepare performance reports for the client, and to troubleshoot the client's advertising setup. Data is never sold, never used for unrelated purposes and never used to build profiles of individuals.

4. Google user data

MV Digital Ads Tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Ads data is used only to provide advertising management and reporting to the client the account belongs to and is not transferred to third parties except as required by law.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with the client business it belongs to, or with service providers that help MV Digital deliver its services under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

6. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on MV Digital computers with owner-only file permissions. Reports are kept only as long as needed for client reporting or as required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

A client can revoke access at any time by unlinking MV Digital from their Google Ads account or Meta Business Manager. Access granted through Google sign-in can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request MV Digital deletes data obtained through these APIs within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.

8. Your rights

Where personal data is involved, individuals have rights under the EU GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and objection. Requests are handled together with the client business acting as data controller.

9. Changes

MV Digital may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to MV Digital using the contact details provided in your service agreement.
